---

# {{$frontmatter.order}} {{ $frontmatter.title }}

Some symbols in the Alternate Symbol Table can be overlaid with an alphanumeric character even when specified in the AX.25 Destination Address. This allows for further customization and identification of stations or objects. Overlays are only available for certain symbols, as marked in the symbol tables.
