title: Overlays with Symbols in the AX.25 Information Field
---

# {{$frontmatter.order}} {{ $frontmatter.title }}

Some symbols in the Alternate Symbol Table can be overlaid with an alphanumeric character when specified in the AX.25 Information field. This allows for additional differentiation, such as indicating a specific vehicle number or type. Overlays are only available for certain symbols, as marked in the symbol tables.
